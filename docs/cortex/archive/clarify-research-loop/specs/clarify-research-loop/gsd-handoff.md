## Objective

Implement a pilot of the seven-terminal-state model for the Cortex pre-spec phase, layered as a refinement on top of the existing `/cortex-spec` necessity gate (BUILD/NARROW/DEFER/REJECT) without modifying the necessity gate itself. The pilot consists of: (1) a mandatory `--terminal {name}` flag on `/cortex-close` that records which of seven terminal states a slug closed at, (2) a new `current-understanding.md` artifact template auto-generated by `/cortex-clarify` from the brief's `initial_terminal_set` frontmatter, and (3) a documented vocabulary of seven terminals with mapping to existing necessity-gate verdicts. A pre-pilot retroactive audit of historical necessity verdicts is a hard gate before any code is written. The slug dogfoods the new flow on itself by closing via `/cortex-close --terminal commit-to-build` once complete.
