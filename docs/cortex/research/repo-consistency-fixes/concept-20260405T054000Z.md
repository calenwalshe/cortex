## Finding 3: .planning/ Ownership Contradiction

### Evidence

**CORTEX.md** (3 locations):
- Line 50: "Hard constraint: Cortex never writes to `.planning/`. GSD owns all workflow state."
- Line 59: "Cortex does not touch `.planning/` during this stage."
- Line 103: "Cortex never writes to `.planning/`"

**cortex-bridge/SKILL.md** (lines 71-446):
- Generates: `.planning/PROJECT.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `REQUIREMENTS.md`, `STATE.md`, `config.json`, phase directories with `CONTEXT.md`

### Root Cause

cortex-bridge was added after the layer architecture was documented. The architecture statement is aspirational; the bridge is the intentional exception.

### Resolution

Replace "Cortex never writes to .planning/" with:

> Cortex skills do not write to `.planning/` during the intelligence lifecycle (clarify, research, spec, execute, validate, repair, assure). The one exception is `/cortex-bridge`, which generates a GSD `.planning/` scaffold from Cortex artifacts as an explicit handoff step. This is a one-time rendering, not ongoing ownership — GSD owns `.planning/` after the bridge runs.

## Finding 4: Eval Gate Ordering — Resolvable

### Evidence

**EVALS.md** (line 103):
> "Contracts without an eval plan reference are incomplete and must not advance past `spec` state."

**Contract template** (line 94):
> "Set to 'pending' until the eval plan is written; contracts cannot advance to done with 'pending'"

**What /cortex-spec actually does** (from SKILL.md):
- Writes `eval_plan` field as `docs/cortex/evals/{slug}/eval-plan.md (pending)`
- Contract is written with `(pending)` — this IS advancing past spec state

### The Contradiction

EVALS.md says contracts with pending eval plans "must not advance past spec state." But /cortex-spec creates contracts WITH pending eval plans and advances the state to `spec` mode. In practice, every contract we've shipped has `(pending)` eval plans and works fine.

### Resolution

EVALS.md is too strict. The contract template is correct: eval_plan can be `(pending)` during spec, but must be resolved before the contract can **close** (advance to done). Fix EVALS.md to say:

> Every active contract must include an `eval_plan` field. The field may be set to `(pending)` during spec and execute phases. Contracts cannot advance to `done` state while eval_plan is `(pending)`.

This matches what /cortex-spec actually does and what the contract template documents.
